01 / PROFILE

Building systems that move a region forward.

His journey brings engineering discipline, commercial thinking and public-purpose leadership together.

Zwelibanzi Sapula is a South African executive working at the intersection of development finance, infrastructure and regional integration.

As Chief Executive Officer of the SADC Development Finance Resource Centre, he works with development finance institutions across Southern Africa to strengthen capability, collaboration and investable regional projects.

Before joining SADC-DFRC in November 2024, his work at the Development Bank of Southern Africa included programme development and major initiatives spanning student housing, water infrastructure, electricity distribution and project preparation.
